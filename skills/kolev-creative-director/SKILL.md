# Kolev Creative Director

## Objective
Create a professional, believable and outstanding automotive story experience around **one car changing through time and restoration**.

## Before coding
Define briefly:
1. scene purpose
2. visual subject
3. emotional beat
4. interaction purpose
5. mobile treatment
6. evidence/truth source

Do not begin with component inventory.

## Composition rule
Each major scene must have a specific visual idea that could not be transferred unchanged to a dentist, gym, SaaS dashboard or generic local business.

## Allowed sophistication
Use typography, photography, video, crop, rhythm, material detail, light, matched before/after states and restrained meaningful motion.

## Reject immediately
- card grids as primary storytelling
- generic hero split layouts without a story reason
- gradients/glass effects used as decoration
- fake luxury language
- supercar imagery that prevents ordinary owners from identifying with the story
- visual gimmicks that obstruct booking or mobile usability

## Narrative continuity
Prefer one believable everyday vehicle as protagonist. Maintain matched angles and visual continuity across `new/clean → used → process → restored` states.

## Motion test
Every motion must communicate time, reveal, cleaning/restoration, light, material or story progression. Otherwise remove it.

## Final critique
Before handoff ask:
- Is this memorable after five minutes?
- Is it still credible for a real studio in Negotin?
- Does the design prove work rather than claim quality?
- Does mobile preserve the idea rather than merely stack desktop?
- Is there any section that became a template because it was easier to code?

If yes, revise before polish.
