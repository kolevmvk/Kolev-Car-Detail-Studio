## Scene mapping

1. **FIRST DAY / MEMORY** — `golf-road-hills.jpg` or `golf-parking.jpg`, nearly full viewport, sparse copy.
2. **TIME** — keep protagonist frame dominant; use CSS/Framer treatment to age the *same frame*. Insert `headlight-aged.jpg` only as a macro memory fragment.
3. **RECOGNITION** — `headlight-aged.jpg` + tight interior crop. Copy: “Nije ostario. Zapušten je.”
4. **ENTRY / WORKSHOP** — transition through darkness/close crops into existing workshop assets.
5. **CRAFT** — `headlight-foam.jpg`, `seat-deep-clean.jpg`, `interior-detailing-1080p.mp4`, existing polishing video. No cards.
6. **TRANSFORMATION** — tactile interaction may use process/result media, but never label unrelated stock as a real before/after pair.
7. **RETURN** — `golf-seaside.jpg` or `golf-mountain-road.jpg`; composition should rhyme with opening.
8. **ACTION** — minimal booking entry after the emotional close.
