# Public Story — One Car, One Life, One Return

## Story premise
The public homepage follows **one believable everyday car** from the memory of being new, through years of ordinary use and unnoticed decline, into the Kolev Car Detailing studio and back out restored.

Do not romanticize neglect. Do not claim mechanical rejuvenation. This is about visible condition, cleanliness, clarity and renewed pride.

## Narrative acts

### Act 1 — When it was new
Open with confidence, not sales copy. The car is visually desirable and clean. Establish its material qualities: clear headlights, clean seats, intact textures, sharp reflections.

Possible copy territory:
- `Sećaš se kako je izgledao kada si ga stvarno gledao?`
- `Nije uvek izgledao umorno.`

No price and no service grid yet.

### Act 2 — Time is gradual
Show accumulated ordinary use rather than dramatic abuse. Opaque headlight, textile darkening, stains, dirt in seams, tired plastics. The core insight:

`Nije se promenio odjednom. Samo si prestao da primećuješ.`

Use visual continuity. Prefer same vehicle and matched angles.

### Act 3 — Recognition
Create a quiet turning point. The visitor should realize that “old-looking” and “irreversible” are not the same thing.

Copy territory:
- `Godine ostaju u saobraćajnoj. Tragovi ne moraju.`
- `Ne vraćamo kilometre. Vraćamo utisak.`

Avoid absolute restoration claims.

### Act 4 — Workshop / craft
The car enters the studio. The visual language becomes tactile and process-focused.

Headlights:
1. condition detail
2. preparation
3. abrasion/polish/protection as applicable
4. final optical clarity
5. night/beam proof when available

Interior:
1. real condition
2. textile/material detail
3. extraction/process
4. dirty-water/process evidence when representative
5. same-angle final result

Keep technique accurate to the real studio workflow once the owner provides it.

### Act 5 — Return
Mirror an early establishing shot. The same car now reads differently.

The emotional target is not “brand new”; it is **I want to look at and drive this car again**.

Possible ending territory:
- `Isti auto. Drugi utisak.`
- `Ne vraćamo vreme. Vraćamo ono što je vreme sakrilo.`

### Act 6 — From story to action
Only after proof is established, make booking dominant.

The CTA should connect directly to the visitor's car:
- `Pogledaj prvi slobodan termin`
- `Pošalji fotografije svog auta`

If availability exists, show real nearest valid slots. If assessment is needed, route to photo submission instead of forcing booking.

## Supporting real cases
After the primary story, real completed jobs form an **Archive**, not a generic gallery.

Each case should contain:
- case number
- vehicle/model/year when owner/customer permits
- starting condition
- services performed
- duration
- before/process/after media
- products/tools used when relevant
- technical partner attribution when factual

## Live studio layer
A small “Now in studio” or current-work element may be used if backed by actual job state. Never fabricate activity. It should make the studio feel alive without turning the page into a dashboard.

## Copy tone
Serbian first. Direct, restrained, confident. Avoid:
- “vrhunski kvalitet”
- “najbolji u gradu”
- “profesionalna usluga” as empty claims
- exaggerated luxury language

Show craft instead of claiming it.
